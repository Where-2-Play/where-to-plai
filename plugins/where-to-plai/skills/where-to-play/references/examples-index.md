# Worked Examples — Index

> **Purpose:** A catalogue of the worked Where-to-Play examples **bundled in this plugin**, so an agent can load the right one just-in-time (few-shot, output template). Read the original file; do not summarize it away.
> **Provenance:** © Sharon Tal & Marc Gruber, from the authors' teaching material (wheretoplay.co).
> **Related:** the Worksheet 1–3 specs (in the `opportunity-set`, `attractiveness-map`, `agile-focus` skills) and `adjacent-concepts.md`.

---

## Bundled examples

### Inergio — complete Market Opportunity Navigator
- **File:** `examples/inergio-full-navigator.md`
- **Illustrates:** **All three worksheets** as a finished one-pager — the canonical "what a completed Navigator looks like." Best reference for the visual scoring mechanic of Worksheet 2 (X-marks on the Low/Mid/High/Super High scales) and a filled Agile Focus Dartboard.
- **Context:** Swiss deep-tech startup, portable autonomous power systems (portable SOFC fuel cell); the MOOC's running flagship case.
- **Use for:** an end-to-end output template; how the three worksheets connect.

### Augury — Worksheet 2 six-factor walkthrough
- **File:** `../../attractiveness-map/references/examples/augury-ws2.md`
- **Illustrates:** **Worksheet 2** scored step by step across **all six factors** with reasoning and real figures (Overall Potential High, Overall Challenge Mid).
- **Context:** Predictive maintenance via machine acoustics; detailed market = HVAC in commercial buildings.
- **Use for:** the strongest few-shot for the Potential/Challenge factor scoring.

### Ceal — Worksheet 1 opportunity set
- **File:** `../../opportunity-set/references/examples/ceal-ws1.md`
- **Illustrates:** **Worksheet 1** output — de-linked core abilities and the re-linked `application × customer` opportunities.
- **Context:** Carbon removal at scale — electrochemical CO₂ removal from seawater; the authors' "most recent case."
- **Use for:** how a finished opportunity set (abilities → applications → customers) reads.
