**Company:** Inergio — *"A new generation of power packs based on highly efficient fuel-cell technology. Delivering clean, reliable electricity for off-grid and backup applications."*

---

## WORKSHEET 1 — Generate Your Market Opportunity Set

### Core abilities / technological elements

| Ability | Functions & properties |
|---|---|
| **Mobile power supply** | Based on Solid Oxide fuel-cell tech · Modular and scalable (20–2500 W) · Compact, light weight · Efficient (4× more than conventional generators) · Reliable and long lasting (15K hours) |
| **Fuel flexibility** | Commercial & standard fuels: Propane, LPG, Natural gas · Green energy carriers: hydrogen, biofuels, ammonia, ethanol |
| **Autonomous operation** | Any weather condition (-20° to 50°) · Any altitude (up to 3K meter) · Standalone (off-grid) or integrated into a hybrid system · Remote monitoring software |
| **User friendly** | Plug & play installment · Easy to refill · Low maintenance · Low capex · Eco friendly: clean & quiet |

### Identified applications × customers (re-linking)

Each application is zoomed in to candidate customer segments; **circled** segments were promoted into the evaluated set.

- **Off-grid monitoring** (distant / scattered locations)
  - *Remote monitoring systems:* Telecom · Weather stations · **⃝ Oil & gas pipelines**
  - *Transportable monitoring systems:* Security systems · **⃝ Traffic management systems** · Events equipment
- **On-the-go energy supply**
  - *Autonomous:* Drones · Robotics
  - *Ultra portable:* Leisure · **⃝ RVs** · Yachts · Smartphones
- **Backup power** (stationary)
  - *Very high power:* **⃝ Data centers** · Cars · Neighborhoods
  - *High power:* House holds

### The Market Opportunity Set (4 opportunities carried forward)

1. **Mobile power supply for traffic management systems**
2. **Mobile power supply for oil & gas remote monitoring**
3. **On-the-go energy supply for RVs**
4. **Backup power for data centers**

## WORKSHEET 2 — Attractiveness Map (evaluate each opportunity)

Each factor placed on **Low → Mid → High → Super High**. Scores below transcribe the hand-placed "X" marks on each worksheet; an X on the line between two levels is recorded as the half-step (e.g. Mid–High).

### 2a. Mobile power supply for traffic management systems  ← **Primary**

| Dimension | Factor | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Potential | Compelling reason to buy | **Super High** |
| Potential | Market volume | High |
| Potential | Economic viability | High |
| **Potential** | **Overall Potential** | **High** |
| Challenge | Implementation obstacles | Mid |
| Challenge | Time to revenues | Low |
| Challenge | External risks | Mid |
| **Challenge** | **Overall Challenge** | **Mid** |

Annotations from the worksheet:
- **Compelling reason to buy:** "Need: relatively high power (20-50W), 24/7 uninterrupted service. Current solutions (batteries + solar panel) offer low capex but high operational cost (opex). Inergio solution allows for better service and lower maintenance costs, while matching the standard energy ecosystem."
- **Market volume:** "Around 150,000 new installations per year (globally). Device sold at 10,000 Euros => a total market of around 1.5 Billion Euros. Road maintenance investments are expected to modestly grow in the next 3-5 years."
- **Economic viability:** "The estimated average operational cost per traffic management system is around 6000 Euros per year. Saving these costs provides high value and allows for a price tag with clear margins. A renting model is also optional for smaller companies."
- **Implementation obstacles:** "Challenges across all applications: manufacturing at scale (hardware) + building credibility and trust with customers. Sales: one customer = hundreds of deployments, but geographically limited. Funding is available."
- **Time to revenues:** "Market is ready (matches the existing energy ecosystem). Sale cycle is expected to take few months at the beginning, and shorter once referrals exist."
- **External risks:** "There is one fuel-cell competitor in this market (SFC Energy), so no need to educate the customers. Inergio's solution has higher lifespan + works with commercial standard fuel. It is patent protected. Regulation currently limits the competitor which works with Methanol. No barriers to adoption."

## WORKSHEET 3 — Design Your Agile Focus Strategy

**I. Primary Market Opportunity:** *Mobile power supply for traffic management systems.*

**II. Other attractive opportunities examined** (relatedness to the primary, on a Low → Mid → High overlap scale):

| Candidate | Product relatedness | Market relatedness | Backup? | Growth? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Other transportable monitoring markets (security, events etc) | High | High | — | ✓ Growth |
| Mobile power supply for oil & gas remote monitoring | High | Mid | ✓ Backup | ✓ Growth |
| On-the-go supply for RVs | Mid | Low | — | ✓ Growth |

*Product relatedness = shared technological competences, required resources, networks. Market relatedness = shared values & benefits, sales channels, word-of-mouth.*

**III. Agile Focus decisions:** all three examined options → **Keep open** (each kept as a Growth option; oil & gas also serves as the Backup option).

### Agile Focus Dartboard (final placement)

| Ring | Market opportunities |
|---|---|
| **Pursue now** (center) | Mobile power supply for traffic management systems |
| **Keep open** (middle ring) | Oil & gas remote monitoring (Backup · Growth) · Other transportable monitoring markets (Growth) · On-the-go energy supply for RVs (Growth) |
| **Place in storage** (outer ring) | Backup power for data centers |

> Logic: the primary (Gold Mine) is pursued now; high-relatedness attractive options are kept open as backup/growth; the low-potential data-centers option (Questionable) is stored.
