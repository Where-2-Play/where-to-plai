# Revisit Guide — the breathing loop

The Navigator is a **breathing document** — a "constant companion" you refine, revise, and adapt as you learn, "re-calculating the route" on new information. This guide details the mechanics behind the `revisit` skill.

## Re-evaluation triggers

Re-open the worksheets when:
- **New evidence arrives** — customer interviews, market data, a competitor move, a regulatory change.
- **An assumption is tested** — confirmed or refuted. Either outcome is progress; learning an assumption is wrong now, before "sweat, tears and money," is a win.
- **Running out of money** ("oxygen") — the runway forces a decision before all data is in.
- **A branding / identity decision looms** — imprinting choices are hard to undo, so revisit focus first.
- **Periodic cadence** — a standing review even absent a shock.

## Assumption-map mechanics

`assumptions.md` plots each open assumption on two axes:
- **X = Evidence**, running from "have evidence" on the left to "no evidence" on the right · **Y = Importance** (how much does it matter).
- Focus the learning journey on the **top-right**: important assumptions with little evidence.
- As evidence comes in, move the assumption left and propose how its confidence colour should change (🔴→🟠→🟢, or back); the team sets it. A refuted assumption is resolved too — record it and flag the factor for re-scoring by the team.

## Keep history — never silently overwrite

The core discipline: prior human judgement is **data**, not noise.
- When a score changes, **append** the change with: date, old value + old reasoning, new evidence, new value. Do not delete the old reasoning.
- At the top of each revisited worksheet, keep a short **changelog**: `YYYY-MM-DD — trigger — what changed`. Date your updates and save them so you can always go back for a review.
- This makes the navigator show its **history of learning** — the point of moving it out of single-snapshot chatbots into living project files.

## Hand-off

`revisit` only flags and proposes. Route re-scoring to `attractiveness-map` and any change of Primary/Backup/Growth to `agile-focus`. The team makes every call.
