|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Product relatedness** = shared technological competences? required resources? necessary networks?
- **Market relatedness** = shared values/benefits (brand)? sales channels? word of mouth?
- A good **Backup** is related **but does not share the Primary's showstoppers**. A good **Growth** is as attractive and as tightly related as possible.

## Investment levels

For each candidate, the team decides: **Keep open** (~5–10%) · **Pursue now in parallel** with the Primary (only if Primary highly uncertain *and* option tightly related on both axes) · **Place in storage**. Every kept-open option names its role — Backup, Growth, or both.

Pursuing two markets in parallel is quite a dangerous strategy — reserve it for the rare case above. And don't slice resources too thinly across too many bets, or you cannot really progress: flexibility does not mean never choosing.

## The Agile Focus Dartboard

Congratulations — you are now ready to depict your strategy here. A good dartboard gives a clear roadmap for growth alongside a clear plan for risk mitigation: A is your focus, B/C/D de-risk it. Never a single locked-in bet, never a scattered menu.
