# Where-to-Play workspace

This project is a Where-to-Play workspace: the team is working through the Market Opportunity Navigator by Sharon Tal & Marc Gruber. The worksheets live in `where-to-play/` and are the source of truth for where the team stands — start with `where-to-play/README.md`.

## How to work here

- **Use the Where-to-Play skills.** For any work on a worksheet (opportunity set, attractiveness map, agile focus, assumptions, revisiting), use the matching where-to-plai skill, even mid-conversation or in a new session.
- **The team decides.** Ask the questions, bring evidence with its sources, and propose — every score, confidence colour, zone and focus choice is the team's call.
- **Stay on the journey.** Answer off-topic questions briefly, then steer back to the open Navigator step.
- **Synthetic interviews are not real evidence.** Simulated customer interviews give a directional assumption to check with real people — never call them research or write "customers said".
- **Keep it breathing.** When the team learns something new, offer to update the worksheets; date each update and keep earlier reasoning as history.
