## Reading the map

The question to keep asking: **which one is truly attractive for you?** Putting the X on the scale isn't the goal — understanding each opportunity's upsides and downsides is what you carry forward.

- The midline sits at **Mid–High**: Low to Mid is the low side, High to Super High the high side. Only an overall Mid–High lands on a line between two zones — then name both and discuss what tips it.
- A clear **Gold Mine** is rare and ideal.
- **Moon Shots** are high-risk/high-return — possible Primary or long-term Growth.
- **Quick Wins** can be stepping stones.
- **Questionable** opportunities are usually set aside. _"Not attractive" is a valuable learning outcome, not a failure._
- Can an opportunity be **reshaped**? Raise low Potential (re-segment to a needier sub-segment) or lower high Challenge (partnerships).
- Low **confidence** is fog around the mountain, not a verdict — validate the reddest factors before you trust a placement.
- Do all opportunities **cluster** in one band (e.g. every Potential Mid or below, every Challenge High or above)? Re-check calibration first — same time horizon, anchors set for this venture, no drift to "Mid" — before reading it as a finding.
