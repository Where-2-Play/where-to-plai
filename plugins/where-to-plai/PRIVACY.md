# Privacy

The Where to PlAI plugin does not collect, store or send any personal data.

## What the plugin does with your data

- **The plugin sends nothing on its own.** It has no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and it contains no code of its own: only Markdown instructions, JSON and an image. Web research goes only through Claude's own tools (below).
- **Your work stays in your project.** The plugin reads and writes Markdown files in the `where-to-play/` folder of your project, and reads notes or research files your team puts into the project. These files stay where you keep them.
- **Web research uses Claude's own tools.** When you ask for market research or customer discovery, Claude searches the web and fetches pages with its built-in `WebSearch` and `WebFetch` tools, under your Claude permissions. The search queries are built from the market opportunity you are researching. Avoid putting confidential details into a research request if you don't want them in a search query.

## Claude and Anthropic

The plugin runs inside Claude Code or Cowork. What you type and what Claude reads from your files is processed by Anthropic under the terms of your Claude plan and [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). The plugin's authors do not receive any of it.
